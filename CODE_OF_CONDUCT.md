# Code of Conduct

cbodotnet is a working community for people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience. We expect everyone participating in project issues,
pull requests and project-controlled discussions to help keep those spaces
professional, welcoming and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while addressing technical claims with
  evidence.
- Critique code, designs and decisions, not a person's identity or worth.
- Make room for newcomers, explain project-specific context, and accept
  constructive review without retaliation.
- Protect private information and keep vulnerability details out of public
  discussion until coordinated disclosure.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation,
unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, retaliation, and repeated disruptive
conduct are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private
communications or identifying information without permission. Spam and
knowingly misleading claims about project support, security or qualification
are also out of scope for healthy project discussion.

## Reporting and Response

Do not argue with or publicly amplify abusive content. Use GitHub's report
content controls on the relevant contribution, or contact
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/) if the concern involves a
maintainer or cannot be reported safely in the repository. Do not use the
private vulnerability reporting channel for non-security conduct reports.
For security-sensitive reports, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers who are not involved in a report will review it, preserve relevant
context, and respond privately where GitHub provides a private channel. They
may issue a warning, remove or hide content, restrict participation in project
spaces, or refer the matter to GitHub for action. Responses should be
proportionate and documented without exposing the reporter's private details.
A maintainer named in the report must not decide its outcome. If no independent
maintainer is available, use GitHub Support rather than treating the complaint
in public.

This policy applies to project-controlled spaces. It does not replace GitHub's
[Community Code of Conduct](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-code-of-conduct)
or Terms of Service.
